SMS READY or not?

The Spot Matching System colour palettes are adapted to be completely seamless whether the colour is process printed (CMYK/CMYKOGV) or if they are displayed digitally on displays or monitors capable of displaying at least 45% NTSC/60% sRGB, preferably 100% of the sRGB colour space.

The colours of all SMS colour palettes, except the new SuperMAX v7 colour palette, are optimized for reproduction/printing in full accordance with ISO 12647-2.

In short, this means that any print shop that is capable of printing correctly according to ISO 12647 within a dE00 of 3 should be qualified for an SMS READY certification for sure.

The difference between a PSO or PSD certification and the SMS READY certification is that in the former case the primary emphasis is on printing of photographs in CMYK, while in the case of the SMS READY certification the focus is only on printing of solid SMS colour halftones/tints printed either in CMYK, XCMYK or CMYKOGV.

SMS READY certified print shops are entitled to offer their customers to use SMS colours in design to be printed at the print shop, - and they are trained to check incoming jobs to make sure that all SMS colours are correct, before going to press, - and how to use the SMS READY control strip to check if the job is correctly prepared for the destination substrate, whether there are SMS colours included within the job or not.

See further information for print shops at www.spotmatchingsystem.com/printers and www.spotmatchingsystem.com/services.

Printshops (Offset, Flexo, Gravure, Digital)

ISO 12647-2/3

ISO 12647-3 is the global standard for coldset, newspaper printing. Our SMS ECO colours are optimized for newspaper printing to this standard. Newspaper printers interested in offering printing of SMS ECO colours to their customers, please contact support@spotmatchingsystem.com for assistance.

ISO 12647-2 is the default printing standard for CMYK offset printing worldwide and consequently the printing standard to be used for correct reproduction of SMS ECO, Standard and MAX colours.

Most printers will be familiar with the FOGRA39, FOGRA47, FOGRA51, FOGRA52 or GRACoL CRPC3 and CRPC6 in the case of G7 printers. Designers may be more familiar with names of icc profiles, such as ISO Coated v2 or PSO Coated v3 etc.
ISO Coated v2 is simply the name of the icc profile created for printing to FOGRA39 and PSO Coated v3 is the name of the icc profile that is made for printing to FOGRA51.

Print shops in all categories of printing (not only offset) that are able to receive PDF documents prepared using one or more of the supported ICC profiles for SMS colours, either for direct output and printing according to ISO 12647-2 or for CMYK-to-CMYK conversion to an in-house printing standard resulting in the same colour outcome, - visually identical to a certified proof according to ISO 12647-7-2016 for the respective substrate are qualified to be SMS READY.

Our goal is not to make things difficult for Print shops.

Quite the opposite actually. Our goal is to make life as easy as utterly possible for brand owners, designers/print customers and print shops.

To keep things as easy as possible for the customers of the printshops, SMS READY certified print shops have to be able to receive PDF's that contain SMS colour in CMYK format with one of the supported, standard icc profiles embedded to the PDF.

At the design side there should be at least one SMS READY Expert to verify that all SMS colours within the layout are correct, once they have been converted to the CMYK destination gamut.

This should be enough information for an SMS READY print shop to know what papertype to use for the job and what colours the customer expects.

Again, print shops are free to use their in-house icc profiles and fingerprint even each press for itself. However, each press/printer has to be up to the task of printing our SMS colours correctly on the requested substrate, which the embedded CMYK icc profile dictates.

This however does not mean that print shops that print on non-standard substrates cannot print SMS colours on  those substrates. As long as the colour gamut is large enough, an SMS READY printer can print on any substrate they wish.

SMS READY certified print shops can even supply their customers with custom icc profiles and assist them (if necessary) to convert their SMS colour correctly to the custom CMYK icc profile for each job.

The SMS READY control strip (see picture above) can always be checked against the supplied CMYK icc profile to make sure that it is in gamut.

The condition for the SMS READY certificate remains the same, regardless of substrate - a MAX dE00 or 3 or less for the SMS colour palette the print shop is certified for.

In general
It is not a condition that the print shop has a formal PSO/PSD or G7 certification but we recommend formal certifications for print shops, just like we recommend taking advantage of the best available solutions available for process control in prepress and on press for best possible outcome.

A lot of companies insist on printing to ISO 12647 standards and it would simply not make sense for a print shop that is capable of printing the SMS colours NOT to offer printing to ISO 12647 standards, since that is only a simple matter of doing a quick in-house colour conversion from - say FOGRA39 to the in-house standard for the respective press using a CMYK-to-CMYK device link icc profile.

This may sound awfully expensive and terribly time consuming, but actually any print shop can do this in-house during down time in prepress and the cost of the equipment required to create such device link icc profiles is only about EUR 7.000 or even less if you shop around. Spot-Nordic for instance offers solutions to do this - see https://www.spot-nordic.com/qc - or feel free to contact info@spot-nordic.com to discuss your options.

You can take as much time as you like to create your device link icc profiles and once you are ready to get certified, just send us your measurements and we will issue the SMS READY certificate, valid for 12 months for your print shop.

It is simply up to each print shop to decide whether they are ready to commit to keeping SMS colours within a MAX dE2000 of 3, a MAX dE of 2 or even less. This depends a lot on the printing process but dE00 of 3 is the red line.

Print shops in all sectors of printing, interested in offering printing of SMS colours may want to consider buying the i1 Pro 3 profiling solution that Spot-Nordic offers to customers worldwide - see www.spot-nordic.com/qc, which is in our opinion the easiest and least expensive way to adjust printers and presses, analog and digital to universal standards, - enabling the same colour output from all your printers and presses.

We have put together appropriate icc profiling packages for print shops  here and here for evaluation.

But to be on the safe side, contact info@spot-nordic.com and have a discussion about what substrates you are working with before you decide which option is right for you.

If you are confident your print shop is all set to go and that printing SMS colours will not be a problem at all, feel free to order your SMS READY certification.

You can then ask us to provide you with the correct SMS READY control strips ready for the print standards you are offering (typically Fogra or G7) - or use the opportunity and have one employee (the person that will be responsible for checking SMS colours and will be our contact person) first do the included SMS READY Expert training and then have him or her prepare the SMS READY control strips - and we will of course be right there to make sure that everything is perfect.

If in doubt, you can download our free SMS control strips here, convert them to your CMYK icc profile in Adobe Photoshop using Absolute Colorimetric Rendering Intent, print them and when you have reached the dE00 you are comfortable with on the presses where SMS colours will be printed, purchase your SMS READY certification - or order it from us. SMS READY certification for product manufacturers

Digital Displays/Monitors/TV displays/LED displays       

Manufacturers in these categories that manufacture digital displays that can be calibrated with display calibrators such as the Calibrite or Spyder display calibrators and are capable of displaying at least the Home & Office version of the SMS colours - requiring the physical colourspace of the display to be at least 45% NTSC/60% sRGB (for a certification for the ECO (e), Standard Home & Office (o) and MAX Home & Office (xo)) - or 100% of the sRGB gamut for a full range SMS READY certificate - ensuring correct displaying of all 5 colour palettes of the Spot Matching System, can apply for an SMS READY certificate for their displays.

Contact support@spotmatchingsystem.com for more information and assistance.

Colourserver + RIP / Workflow server

Manufacturers of workflow servers, that are capable of outputting PDF files prepared with the icc profiles included for reproduction of SMS colours for either direct or indirect output can apply for an SMS READY certificate.

Contact support@spotmatchingsystem.com for more information and assistance.

Colourserver + RIP + Printer/Press + (optional) substrate.         

Manufacturers of Colourservers, digital printers/digital presses/proofing devices + optionally substrates, that are capable of handling PDF files prepared with icc profiles included for reproduction of SMS colours for direct output and consistent printing of SMS colour to standards within a combined MAX dE00 of 2 or less can apply for an SMS READY certificate.

Contact support@spotmatchingsystem.com for more information and assistance.

Inks, dyes and paint (any material)         

Manufacturers in these categories that have the technology and capacity to commit to producing a recipe and a colour sample (if requested), ink, dye or paint which, when applied to the intended material, will match any requested SMS colour, visually and instrumentally, in D50 or D65 light, with a MAX dE2000 difference of 1 or less, once the sample is dry, can apply for an SMS READY certificate for their products.

Contact support@spotmatchingsystem.com for more information and assistance.

Paper/substrates suited for printing

Papertypes that are consistent with papertypes suited for printing of SMS colours - see here, that the manufacturer commits to manufacture with a white point within a dE2000 of 2 or less from the respective standard white point can apply for an SMS READY certificate.

Contact support@spotmatchingsystem.com for more information and assistance.

Printer/digital press + Paper/Media        

Any combination of a Printer + Paper/Media capable of reproducing either SMS ECO, SMS Standard or SMS MAX colours within a MAX dE00 of 2 or less, is qualified for an SMS READY certificate.

The certification can be issued both to the manufacturer or to a qualified reseller of the Printer or the media (SMS READY Expert training required).

Contact support@spotmatchingsystem.com for more information and assistance.

Product Manufacturers         

Product manufacturers in any category, that are capable of consistently reproducing products dyed in SMS colours within a MAX dE of 2 (colour measured with a 0/45 instrument at either D50 or D65 and compared to the SMS master LAB values) can apply for an SMS READY certificate.

Contact support@spotmatchingsystem.com for more information and assistance.

QC software and hardware   

Software and hardware (including spectral and density measurement devices) that support and aid with consistent printing of SMS colours can apply for an SMS READY certificate.
Spectral measurement devices need to measure any SMS colour within a dE00 of 2 or less from the actual LAB value of the printed sample to be considered.

Contact support@spotmatchingsystem.com for more information and assistance.

Software Manufacturers         

Software manufacturers in any category, that wish to include the colour palettes of the Spot Matching System within their software can apply for an SMS READY certificate.

Spectrophotometers/spectrodensitometers         

Spectrophotometers / Spectro Densitometers that are capable of consistently measuring SMS colours within an accuracy and MAX dE of 0,2 (0/45 instrument at either D50 or D65 and colours compared to the SMS master LAB values) can apply for an SMS READY certificate.

Contact support@spotmatchingsystem.com for more information and assistance.

Proofing facilities   

Proofing facilities that provide contract proofs on Fogra or G7 certified proofing media (Fogra 39, Fogra 47, Fogra 51 and Fogra 52 / CRPC3 and CRPC6) printed on a minimum of 4-up proofing device with an inline measurement device for certification according to ISO 12647-7-2016, may apply to enter into a service agreement with Spot-Nordic to print and provide SMS standard and custom proofs to SMS customers in their vicinity.

Maximum allowed dE2000 between our SMS target colours and proof is 2 or less.  A handheld M0/M1/M2 enabled spectral device is required on location to take random measurements to ensure correct and consistent proof production to SMS standards.

Once these conditions have been fulfilled the proofing facility is considered SMS READY and an appropriate SMS READY certificate will be issued to the proofing facility outlining their print standards.

Contact support@spotmatchingsystem.com for more information and assistance.
