SMS READY - or not?


Experts

The SMS READY Expert diploma program was launched in the summer of 2024, following the release of version 6 of the Spot Matching System colour palettes.

The purpose of the program, which is technical training in the use of SMS colours from design to reproduction, is to prevent mistakes that can occur, both when converting SMS colours from one colour space to another, as well as in reproduction of SMS colours, due to misunderstanding or mistakes on the design side.

SMS READY Experts should always be present on both the design and the reproduction side of the workflow to ensure that each SMS colour is correctly created and reproduced.

SMS READY Experts come from various backgrounds and can offer new SMS users their services - either simply as "super users" to double check SMS colours for design and/or reproduction on behalf of design agencies, brand owners, printshops or manufacturers or to do seminars, for onsite or remote training for groups that prefer onsite training to remote training to get their own SMS READY Expert diploma or other specialized and industry specific purposes - onsite or remotely or even to be qualified to sell the Spot Matching System in their region.

SMS READY Expert training is available in the field of PRINT GRAPHICS, WEB GRAPHICS and VIDEO GRAPHICS.

What SMS READY Experts have in common is that they are open to push the boundaries of colour technology and colour consistency - which is exactly what the Spot Matching System is all about.
